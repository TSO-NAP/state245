NON-AGGRESSION POLICY

Tiles Survive! — State #245

This document defines the only protected targets in State #245.

This is not a negotiated agreement and is not open to interpretation.

1. Scope of Protection

Player Settlements and active mines located in the wilderness.

This is the full and final scope of protection.

2. What Is NOT Protected

Any additional protection unnecessarily complicates a game designed as a war game.

Extra rules are difficult to enforce, constantly abused, and destroy gameplay.

No further protections exist.

3. No Rule Expansion

If something is not explicitly protected, it is not protected.

4. Violations and Consequences

The alliance of the attacked party may retaliate by burning settlements or taking decisive military action.

No warnings are guaranteed.

5. Final Statement

Assume it is allowed.
