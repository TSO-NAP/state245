NON-AGGRESSION POLICY

Tiles Survive! — State #245

This document defines the only protected targets under the Non-Aggression Policy currently enforced in State #245.

This is not a negotiated agreement, not a mutual contract, and not subject to discussion, interpretation, or expansion.

1. Scope of Protection

The following targets are protected and must not be attacked:

• Player Settlements located in the wilderness

This is the full and final scope of protection.

2. What Is NOT Protected

Any attempt to extend protection beyond the scope defined above unnecessarily complicates the functioning of the game.

The game is designed as a war game. Additional non-aggression rules are:

• Difficult or impossible to enforce
• Constantly disputed and exploited
• Destructive to gameplay, conflict, and long-term enjoyment

No additional protections exist.

3. No Rule Expansion

There will be no exceptions, no special cases, and no interpretation based on intent, feelings, or custom.

If something is not explicitly protected, it is not protected.

4. Violations and Consequences

Any deliberate attack against a protected target constitutes a violation.

In such a case, the alliance of the attacked party reserves the right to respond by burning settlements or taking other decisive military action against the violator.

Enforcement is not guaranteed to include warnings and is not subject to negotiation.

5. Purpose

This policy exists solely to prevent pointless destruction of settlements while preserving meaningful, unrestricted warfare elsewhere.

It represents the minimum limitation required for a functional and competitive state.

6. Final Statement

If you are unsure whether an action is allowed:

Assume it is allowed.

This policy is effective immediately and remains in force until officially replaced.
